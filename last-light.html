SURVIVOR'S FIELD GUIDE

Every action buys time.

01 / Your turn

Start with 4 AP. Select a card, then a highlighted target. Deploy guns in the equipment row. Select a gun, then an enemy to fire. Each gun fires once per turn; reloading does not reset it. Press Escape or Cancel to clear a selection.

02 / Keep the doors standing

Enemy attack minus barricade armor damages the barricade. At zero durability it breaks. On its next activation an approaching enemy enters the breach instead of attacking. Only later activations damage your survivor. Repair intact barriers; rebuild an empty breach.

03 / Read their intentions

Each enemy shows its next action. Delays skip activations. A living Chorus grants other enemies +1 attack. Gunfire can target any lane; the Breach Gun and Wrecking Bar require a breached enemy.

04 / The night phase

Existing enemies activate in lane order, breached enemies first. Then the horde attempts to spawn. New arrivals cannot attack that phase. One attempt per turn initially; missions 2–3 use two after turn 3.

D6 spawn chances: Drifter 5/6, Rushling 4/6, Ironback and Chorus 3/6. A third attempt is guaranteed after two failed rolls. A full approach row pauses spawning without removing cards.

05 / Supplies and victory

A 20-card base deck + 5 ally cards. Draw 6 initially, with a guaranteed long-range firearm. Draw 2 each new turn; hand limit 8. Discard reshuffles when needed. Deployed gear stays out of the draw pile. Rations exhaust for the battle.

Clear all active enemies and the finite horde to win. Lose at 0 survivor HP. Health and barricades reset between missions. Choose one reward, edit your deck, and move on. Local saves resume on this browser.
